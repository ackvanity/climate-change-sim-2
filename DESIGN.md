# Design Tokens

## Colors

**Note**: Why not red?

Red _is_ a nice color for climate change - it's the natural color for danger after all, but the thing is, the red here is pretty strong. Moreover, while it is true this is a dangerous issue, I don't see a reason why we ought to scare any would-be users anyway. Instead, use green: it nicely complements the biospheric motivation behind this site.
